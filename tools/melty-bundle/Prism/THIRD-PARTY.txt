UltraCraft ships this copy of Minecraft's launcher setup so ULTRAKILL can start Minecraft by itself.
UltraCraft doesn't include Minecraft: Prism Launcher downloads it after you sign in with
a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.
